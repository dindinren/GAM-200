POST HASTE, M1 Submission

CONTROLS
"W": Walk Right
"A": Walk Left
"Space": Jump 
"Right-Click Mouse ": Drag packages

HOW TO PLAY
- Choose up to 3 packages to get delivering
- You must choose at least 2 to start the day
- Once the packages are chosen, drag them using the mouse onto the green paddle
- You cannot drag them once placed on the paddle

- Once ready, head to the left to start delivering

- At the moment, it is still WIP but walk to the right to deliver to the receipients

- To deliver, click on them
